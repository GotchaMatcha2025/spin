how it works

who runs it

Gotcha Matcha Pte Ltd (UEN 202435893D), The Woodleigh Mall (B1-K16) and Jurong Point (B1-83/85), Singapore.

when and where

on this page, only while our fran x gotcha matcha booth at bugis+ is open: wed 11 nov, 9am–3pm · fri 13 nov, 5pm–8pm · sat 14 nov, 9am–3pm, 2026 (singapore time).

how to play

free, nothing to buy. one spin per person and per phone. for ages 13 and over. spins made by scripts or other tricks may be cancelled.

how prizes are picked

every spin wins one prize. our server picks it at random the moment you tap spin; the wheel just shows it. chance per spin: free cold foam 60%, free upsize 20%, 1-for-1 15%, free drink 5%. each day at most 60 1-for-1s and 20 free drinks can be won; once they're gone that day, they drop out and the other prizes share their chance. these numbers are fixed for the whole event.

the prizes

free cold foam: one of our cold foams, added to a drink you buy. free upsize: regular to large, on a drink you buy. 1-for-1: buy one drink, get a second of the same or lower price free. free drink: any drink, regular size. use at the woodleigh mall or jurong point, sun 15 nov to sun 29 nov 2026, during opening hours. show your voucher (a screenshot is fine) and our staff scan it. one voucher per cup, used once. no cash, no swaps.

your data

we don't ask your name, number or email. we keep a random id for this phone, your prize, your code and when you spun, to keep it to one spin per phone and to check vouchers, plus a scrambled network address to slow down bulk spins (cleared after 30 days). your voucher is also saved on this phone until you clear it. everything we keep is deleted after 29 dec 2026. the whatsapp club is separate and optional: joining shares your number with us for news. questions or deletion: hello@gotchamatcha.sg.

stopping early

if the spin is misused, we may stop it early; vouchers already won still work.
